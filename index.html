What you are buying

3.2 Paying and the exchange rate

You pay in Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, USD Coin, Tether USD, XRP or Monero. After signing in with your wallet you choose a coin and a network and receive a deposit address of your own. On XRP and similar networks the payment must carry the destination tag shown with the address; a payment without it cannot be assigned to you.

Which moment counts. A payment is converted when it is final on its own network, not when you send it: on the network's own finality, or after a fixed number of confirmations on Bitcoin and Monero. The L2P price is the price of the era in which that moment falls.

Which rate. The US dollar value of your coin at that moment is the average of the prices on Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, OKX and Bybit, leaving out any price that deviates too far from the median.

No correction afterwards. The L2P amount calculated at that moment is final. It is not adjusted later, in either direction, whatever happens to the price of your coin or of L2P.

3.3 Delivery

The L2P you buy is credited as a balance on your presale account, the account of the wallet you signed in with. Until the mainnet launch it is an account balance: it cannot be transferred or sold. Its one use before then is that it weighs the account's daily vote in the community election at voting.l2protocol.com.

At the launch of the L2 Protocol mainnet the balance can be claimed through the claim-and-stake contract, either to your own wallet or straight into staking. The launch is planned for [planned moment, e.g. a quarter].

Changing your mind

If you buy as a private individual, you can withdraw from your purchase within 14 calendar days, free of charge and without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day your purchase is credited to your presale account.

How. Send an email to [withdrawal email address], or open a ticket at support.l2protocol.com, stating the wallet address you signed in with, the purchase you are withdrawing from and the address the refund should go to.

Refund. We return the full amount we received for that purchase, in the same coin, within 14 days of receiving your request. We pay the network fee for the refund. The L2P from that purchase is removed from your balance.

When the right ends. The presale has a fixed end. Once it has ended, the right of withdrawal can no longer be exercised, even if fewer than 14 days have passed since your purchase.

Risks

Buy only what you can afford to lose entirely. In particular:

  • You can lose everything. The value of L2P can fall to zero.
  • You may not be able to sell. There is no guarantee that L2P will ever be listed on a trading platform or that anyone will want to buy it from you.
  • The project may not deliver. L2 Protocol is built by a small team and the software is still in development. The mainnet launch can be delayed, or not happen at all.
  • Software and keys. Smart contracts, including the claim-and-stake contract, can contain errors. If you lose access to the wallet you signed in with, you cannot claim your L2P, and nobody can recover it for you.
  • Exchange rate. The price of the coin you pay with can move between the moment you send and the moment your payment is final. That risk is yours.
  • Payment errors. A payment on the wrong network, to the wrong address or without the required destination tag can be lost.
  • Regulation. The rules for crypto-assets are changing. A change in law or in its interpretation can affect the presale, the chain or your ability to hold or sell L2P.
  • No protection scheme. Your purchase is not covered by a deposit guarantee scheme or an investor compensation scheme.
  • Tax. Buying and holding L2P can have tax consequences for you. Private individuals in the Netherlands generally declare it in box 3. Ask a tax adviser about your own situation.
